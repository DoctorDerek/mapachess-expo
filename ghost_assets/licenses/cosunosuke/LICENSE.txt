DoctorDerek asked the creator whether purchasers may use this chess pack
in commercial projects without redistributing the pack as-is, identifying
the intended use as integration into his free-to-play chess game.

Cosunosuke replied:
"Hi! Yes, absolutely!

Integrating the pack into your game is completely fine as long as the raw
asset files aren't redistributed as a standalone package.

Good luck with your project!"

This reply was supplied by the purchasing project owner from the source
page's comment thread. It is specific creator permission, not a grant under
MIT, CC0, Apache, or the application's GPL license.

Runtime use: twelve 32×32 piece cells extracted from the supplied sheet,
preserving the original gold and blue colors, and the unmodified 256×256
frameless board. The source sheet, framed board, selection indicator and
original source ZIP remain private; they are not included in the runtime bundle.

Authorized builds use the existing encrypted chess-art bundle. Encryption
does not grant additional rights. Do not redistribute raw assets as a
standalone package. Creator attribution and this source link are retained
in repository notices and in-game Credits.
